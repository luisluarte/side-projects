# The Cortico-Cerebellar Thermodynamic Sudoku Architecture

**Model Designation:** `CC_Model_069` **Topological Configuration:** Tanh Basis, $\kappa = 0.8$, $K_{sa} = 20$ Jumps

This document outlines the complete mathematical formulation of the optimal predictive model of human choice and chronometrics, dynamically bounding Rademacher complexity through an active thermodynamic submanifold constraint.

## I. Cortical Representation & Granular Expansion

At trial $t$, the sensory and contextual state $\mathbf{x}_t \in \mathcal{X}$ is projected into a high-dimensional continuous Hilbert space $\mathbf{h}_t \in \mathbb{R}^N$ via the cerebellar granular layer. For `CC_Model_069`, the expansion basis is structurally governed by a hyperbolic tangent (Tanh) non-linearity:

$$ \mathbf{h}_t = \tanh(\mathbf{W}_{in} \mathbf{x}_t + \mathbf{b}) $$

Where $\mathbf{W}_{in}$ is a fixed random orthogonal projection matrix.

## II. Thermodynamic Submanifold Masking (The "Sudoku" Engine)

To prevent the high-dimensional expansion from absorbing Rademacher noise, the model applies a dynamic boolean mask $\mathbf{S}_t \in \{0, 1\}^N$ to the granular population.

For exactly $K_{sa} = 20$ micro-steps, the network performs Metropolis-Hastings simulated annealing. At each step $k$: 1. A candidate mask $\mathbf{S}_{k}'$ is generated by flipping a random locus in $\mathbf{S}_k$. 2. The localized likelihood energy $\Delta E = \mathcal{L}_{DDM}(\mathbf{S}_k') - \mathcal{L}_{DDM}(\mathbf{S}_k)$ is computed. 3. The transition is accepted with probability $P(\text{accept}) = \min(1, \exp(-\beta_k \Delta E))$.

The terminal mask $\mathbf{S}_t^*$ aggressively prunes the topological dimensions that do not strictly maximize the scalar evidence density.

## III. Temporal Eligibility Trace Integration

The unmasked granular dimensions are integrated over time by the parallel fibers. `CC_Model_069` dictates an optimal temporal decay scalar of $\kappa = 0.8$:

$$ \mathbf{Z}_t = 0.8 \cdot \mathbf{Z}_{t-1} + \left( \mathbf{S}_t^* \odot \mathbf{h}_t \right) $$

## IV. Deep Cerebellar Nuclei (DCN) Readout

The Purkinje cells decode the surviving eligibility traces via synaptic weights $\mathbf{w}$, yielding a scalar neuromodulatory coupling term $\Omega_t$:

$$ \Omega_t = \mathbf{w}^T \mathbf{Z}_t $$

## V. Basal Ganglia Action-Value Modification

The striatal Q-values (updated via standard Rescorla-Wagner rule) are dynamically gated by the DCN coupling scalar, polarizing the action boundaries:

$$ v_{0, t} = \max(k_v Q_{0, t} - \Omega_t, \epsilon) $$ $$ v_{1, t} = \max(k_v Q_{1, t} + \Omega_t, \epsilon) $$

Where $k_v$ is the base drift-rate scaling parameter.

## VI. The Joint Drift-Diffusion Likelihood ($\mathcal{L}_{DDM}$)

The polarized action values drive the evidence accumulator. The discrete choice probability follows a Softmax continuous readout: $$ P(\text{Choice}_t = ch) = \frac{\exp(v_{ch, t})}{\exp(v_{0, t}) + \exp(v_{1, t})} $$

Simultaneously, the continuous Reaction Time ($RT$) distribution is strictly modeled as the First-Passage Time (FPT) of a Wiener Process (Wald distribution): $$ RT_t \sim \text{Wald}\left( \mu = \frac{a}{v_{ch, t}}, \lambda = a^2 \right) + t_{nd} $$ $$ f(RT_t) = \sqrt{\frac{a^2}{2\pi (RT_t - t_{nd})^3}} \exp \left( - \frac{(a(RT_t - t_{nd}) - \frac{a}{v_{ch, t}})^2}{2 (RT_t - t_{nd})} \right) $$

**The Scalar Objective:** The CMA-ES optimizer continuously searches for parameters $\theta = \{a, t_{nd}, k_v, \alpha_c, \alpha_b, w_{scale}\}$ that strictly minimize the joint Negative Log-Likelihood: $$ \mathcal{L}_{DDM} = - \sum_{t=1}^{T} \left( \log P(\text{Choice}_t) + \log f(RT_t) \right) $$
