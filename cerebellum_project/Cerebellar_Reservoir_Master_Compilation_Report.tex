\section{Detailed Analysis of Core Stage Interventions}

\subsection{Unbounded Logit Transformation \& Boundary Elimination}
The initial manifold optimization suffered from steep boundary clamping ($\text{BCR} = 60\%$) near physical limits, causing optimizer instability and step discontinuities ($R^2_{\text{cubic}} = 0.7817$). 

By transforming parameters into an unbounded latent space $\tilde{\boldsymbol{\Theta}} \in \mathbb{R}^5$ using:
\begin{equation}
\tilde{\Theta}_k = \ln \left( \frac{\Theta_k - \Theta_{\min, k}}{\Theta_{\max, k} - \Theta_k} \right) \quad \Longleftrightarrow \quad \Theta_k = \Theta_{\min, k} + \frac{\Theta_{\max, k} - \Theta_{\min, k}}{1 + \exp(-\tilde{\Theta}_k)}
\end{equation}
we achieved **analytical $\text{BCR} = 0.0000$ (0\% boundary clamping)**, producing continuous polynomial trajectories across $D(\mathbf{W}_{in}) \in [0.01, 0.20]$ and guaranteeing spectral radius sub-criticality ($\lambda_{fb}(0.20) = 0.9783 \le 0.99$).

\subsection{C++ Biophysics \& Tsodyks-Markram Short-Term Depression ($D_t$)}
Un-normalized Mossy Fiber signals containing massive DC offsets ($+10.0$) drive standard reservoirs into severe $\tanh$ saturation ($70.13\%$ of neurons at $|x| > 0.90$), collapsing state variance to $0.7832$. 

Integrating the Tsodyks-Markram differential equation:
\begin{equation}
\frac{dD_t}{dt} = \frac{1 - D_t}{\tau_{\text{rec}}} - U_{\text{SE}} \cdot D_t \cdot u(t)
\end{equation}
dynamically depresses effective input gain ($D_{\text{ss}} \approx 0.0062$), controlling pre-activation ($|h_{\text{pre}}| \le 10.93$), eliminating overshoot saturation (**$0.00\%$ saturation**), and preserving rich linear dynamics ($\text{Var}[x_i] = 0.5636$).

\subsection{Stage 6 \& 7 LOOCV Choice-Latency Benchmarking}
Evaluated across 128 human participants ($16,029$ trials):
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Discrete Choice Prediction}: The discrete Win-Stay / Lose-Shift (WSLS) Markov baseline achieved the optimal out-of-sample fit ($\text{NLL} = 53.25$, $\text{PR-AUC} = 0.6840$). The unpatched continuous reservoir achieved $\text{NLL} = 77.66$, while the patched reservoir achieved $\text{NLL} = 116.99$.
    \item \textbf{Continuous Reaction Time Latency}: Bridging WSLS to a Drift Diffusion Model (WSLS-DDM) yielded a continuous Reaction Time RMSE of $0.5859\text{ s}$ and a total Joint Log-Likelihood of $20,702.2$.
\end{itemize}
